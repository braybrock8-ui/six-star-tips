GTA 6 changes a lot of series habits. Cash can be lost, the police track descriptions instead of just your location, and cars run on fuel. These are the things worth knowing before you get comfortable in Leonida.

> [!TIP] Want it as a to-do list? The [first 48 hours checklist](/checklist/) saves your progress in your browser.

# Protect your cash first

- If you get **Busted or Wasted, you drop all the loose cash you're carrying**. Rockstar described this before launch, and IGN's official guide confirms it.
- Until you can bank, keep spare money in the **stash at Jason's safehouse** (his bedroom).
- The **Buck Me** main mission in Chapter 1 opens a BuckMe bank account. Deposit everything as soon as it's open. Banked money is safe from death and arrest, and both Jason and Lucia can use it.
- BuckMe has a deposit limit that goes up as the story progresses. Its app also has an ATM finder.
- Hit the first **savings goal of $5,000** early. IGN says savings goals pay interest bonuses. GTABoom and PowerPyx warn that money put into Family Savings is locked away for good, so only put in what you won't need.

> [!WARN] Two guides warn against grinding money in Chapter 1. GTABoom reports that cash you're still carrying is wiped when Chapter 1 ends. Bank what you earn and keep moving with the story.

# Learn Focus mode

Click **both sticks** (L3 + R3) to enter Focus. Time slows, colour drains out of the world, and useful things light up:

| Colour | What it means |
|---|---|
| Red | Weapons, explosives and enemies |
| Purple | Drugs and product you can sell |
| Green | Cash |
| White | Things you can interact with, like doors |

Outside combat you get about 30 seconds of Focus on foot. Aim while in Focus to use **Combat Focus**, which unlocks in Chapter 1 and shows weak spots on enemies.

# Small controls that change everything

- **Cross / A is jump** by default. You can change it in the options.
- You set walking speed by **clicking the left stick**.
- **Hold D-pad Down** to slow time and see nearby activities on the map. Click the right stick to set a waypoint without pausing.
- **Heal mid-fight:** hold L1 / LB for the wheel, then press Square / X for Zombix pills (carry up to 5) or Triangle / Y for an Adrenaline syringe, which overfills all three stat bars.
- If a witness pulls out their phone to call the cops, walk up and press **Square / X to slap it out of their hand**.
- **Hit markers** tell you what happened: white means hit, yellow means incapacitated, red means killed.

# Weapons: owned vs unowned

- Guns you pick up from enemies are **unowned**. You lose them for good if you drop them, quit without storing them, or get busted. Store them in your personal vehicle's trunk to keep them.
- Guns you **buy at Ammu-Nation are owned**. You can re-equip them free from weapon lockers at some Ammu-Nation stores.
- **Yellow weapons** are Unique Weapon Variants. They unlock permanently the moment you pick them up.

# Cars need fuel and repairs

- Cars have a **fuel gauge** and a **body condition meter**. Electric cars need charging stations.
- To repair a car, use a Pay 'n' Spray, a Mod Shop, or a gas pump (Services → Repair). Every option costs money, so drive carefully early on.
- Tolls cost **$5**, unless you're in a stolen or borrowed car.
- Newer cars need a **Key Cloner** to steal quietly, and some carry **advanced trackers** that report you to police if you stop moving. You get the Key Cloner later in the story.
- Jason's Key Lento home has no space for extra cars. You get more vehicle storage in **Chapter 2 after meeting Moses**.
- After the main mission **Just a Scratch**, you can call **Joey** to bring your car to you.

# Your first missions and easy money

1. Finish **Gone Fishing**. That's when the open world opens up.
2. **Fix the four projects at Jason's house** (steps, roof, railing, bedroom wall) before you pick Lucia up in *Coming Home*. After that they're gone for good. [Where they are](/guides/gta-6-fix-railing-jasons-house/)
2. Loot the **Kingfishers: Fishing Shacks** operation north-west of Key Lento. A full clear is worth close to $2,000. See the [money guide](/guides/gta-6-make-money-fast/).
3. When Shirley asks for rum, **finish her errand before checking the "strange noises"** behind the Three Sheets liquor store. Investigating first starts a gunfight that locks the shop for a long time.
4. Sell product to **Brian or Donnie** quickly. Hover their map icons first, because one often pays more.

# Stats you can train

Jason and Lucia each have **Health, Stamina and Focus**. Each stat has four levels.

- Weights raise Health. Running, cycling and treadmills raise Stamina.
- Fishing, pool, golf and shooting ranges train Focus.
- Food and drink give temporary boosts. A quick way to buff all three stats is **water and an apple**.

More detail is in [Jason & Lucia: switching, stats and relationship](/guides/gta-6-jason-lucia/).
