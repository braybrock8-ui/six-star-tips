Most open-world games punish you a little for rushing: you spend early money badly, miss things you'd have liked to grab, or burn out on side content before the story gives you the good tools. This guide keeps it simple and spoiler-light.

> [!TIP] Want the short version? Use the [interactive checklist](/checklist/). It saves your progress in your browser.

# Before you start

Spend two minutes in the settings first. It's much easier than changing things mid-mission. Our [settings guide](/guides/gta-6-best-settings/) covers what to change.

# The opening hours

Play the story until the map opens up. Early side content is usually easier once you've got a few story tools.

- [ ] Finish the opening missions
- [ ] Save at your first safehouse
- [ ] Check your phone for contacts and apps
