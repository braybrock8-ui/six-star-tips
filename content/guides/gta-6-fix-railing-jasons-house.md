**Quick answer:** the railing is at the **back** of Jason's Key Lento house, not the front stairs. Go out the back door and down to the **outdoor gym**. Turn around to face the house, and you'll see a gap in the railing **next to a ladder**. Walk up to the gap and interact with it to fix it.

> [!WARN] These projects are **missable**. Once you pick Lucia up in the main mission *Coming Home*, you can't go back and do them. Finish all four first.

# When you can do it

The house projects open up once you've finished **Gone Fishing**, the first mission of Chapter 1, and stay available until *Coming Home*. Rare Thief reports they cost nothing and you don't need to gather materials, because the supplies are already at each spot.

# All 4 projects at Jason's house

| Project | Where | What to do |
|---|---|---|
| Steps | Outside, by the stairs nearest the mailbox and driveway | Interact with the stack of wooden 2x4s (hold Triangle / Y) |
| Leaky roof | Up the yellow ladder at the front porch | Interact with the shingles on the roof |
| Railing | Back of the house by the outdoor gym, next to a ladder | Face the house from the gym and interact with the gap |
| Bedroom wall | Bedroom, on the wall opposite the bed | Interact with the wall frame, put up the drywall, then pick a paint colour |

The bedroom wall takes the longest because of the painting step. The paint colour you pick makes no difference.

# Why bother?

The game says finishing the projects makes Lucia happier, which helps Jason and Lucia's relationship. See [relationship levels](/guides/gta-6-jason-lucia/). Even if you don't care about the romance, the house looks better for the rest of the game.

# Can't find the railing?

Guides describe the landmark differently: a kettlebell, a weight bench or a squat rack. All of those are the **outdoor workout area at the back** of the house. Stand among the gym equipment, turn to face the house, and look for the missing section beside the ladder.

# More to do before Lucia comes home

Chapter 1 has a few other errands for Lucia's return, and these also lock once she's home. They include picking up groceries, buying flowers, getting Lucia a custom necklace, collecting her friend's clothes, and getting a haircut.
