Short answers first, with links to the full guides. We only state something plainly when at least two sources agree.

# Can you play GTA 6 in first person?

**Not fully.** There's no full first-person mode like GTA 5's. You only get first person in two cases:

- **Scoped weapons:** aim with L2 / LT, then click the right stick to look down the scope.
- **Phone while driving:** pulling out your phone in a car switches to a first-person view.

# How do you change the radio station?

In a vehicle, **hold D-pad Left** to open the radio and vehicle quick-menu, then pick a station. A quick tap on D-pad Left only works the turn signal. The same menu works on foot once you own **headphones**, which you can buy at electronics stores. Jason and Lucia also have on-demand podcasts.

# Where do you get a mask?

The first place to buy one is **Chuck's Tackle & Shot** in Key Lento, east of Jason's house. It sells gaiters that double as a mask. Game8 reports bandanas there for $35, and says the store opens for shopping once you've bought a longarm during *Gear Up*.

To put it on, hold **L1 / LB**, tab to items with **R1 / RB**, and pick the mask slot. Covering your face before a crime helps keep the police description vague. See [Wanted & Heat](/guides/gta-6-wanted-heat/).

# How do you save?

PowerPyx reports that you save manually from the **pause menu → Game → Save Game**, with 15 slots and one autosave. You can't save during most missions, and using a cheat turns saving off for that session. [Full saving guide](/guides/gta-6-getting-around/)

# How do you keep a stolen car?

From **Chapter 2** you can register a stolen car as a personal vehicle. Scan a car with the WAiNK app (hold D-pad Up) first to check its security. Cars with trackers are trouble: advanced trackers call the police whenever you stop. Jason's first house has no room for extra cars, and more storage opens up after you meet Moses. [Cars and garages](/guides/gta-6-getting-around/)

# Where do you sell cars?

- **Chapter 2:** sell to **Moses** in Port Gellhorn. He only takes cars without trackers and pays roughly $1,000 to $3,000 each.
- **Chapter 3:** **Pay 'n' Spray** buys cars with standard trackers, and **Randy** pays $30,000+ for wish-list supercars.

There's no car buyer in Chapter 1. [Money guide](/guides/gta-6-make-money-fast/)

# Where is the railing at Jason's house?

At the back of the house, by the outdoor gym, next to a ladder. Fix it before Lucia comes home, or you'll miss it. [Railing guide](/guides/gta-6-fix-railing-jasons-house/)

# Is there a money glitch?

None confirmed so far. Glitches tend to get patched fast and can cost you your save. The fastest legitimate money is bank vaults at the end of Chapter 4, which pay up to $205,000 each. [Money guide](/guides/gta-6-make-money-fast/)

# Is GTA 6 on PS4 or Xbox One?

**No. It's only on PS5 and Xbox Series X|S.** Xbox One owners can **stream** it through Xbox Cloud Gaming, which uses Series S graphics settings. PS4 has no version and no streaming option.

# Is GTA 6 on PC?

**Not yet, and Rockstar hasn't announced a PC version.** Historically, PC versions have arrived 12 to 18 months after console, but that's an analyst's estimate, not a date. [Release facts](/guides/gta-6-release-editions/)

# Is there GTA 6 Online?

**Not at launch.** GTA 6 is single-player. Rockstar hasn't announced an online mode or a date.

# Is there a 60fps mode?

**No.** Rockstar targets 30fps on every console. PS5 Pro gets sharper visuals and ray-traced shadows, not a higher frame rate. [Performance guide](/guides/gta-6-best-settings/)

# How big is the download?

**Up to 212 GB**, depending on your console, plus day-one patches.
