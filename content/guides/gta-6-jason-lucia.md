# Who they are

- **Lucia Caminos** was sent to the Leonida Penitentiary for fighting for her family, and got out by luck. She is voiced by **Manni L. Perez**.
- **Jason Duval** grew up around grifters. After a stint in the Army, he was working for drug runners in the Keys when he met Lucia. He is voiced by **Dylan Rourke**.

# When can you play as Lucia?

- The game opens with Lucia in prison, so **Chapter 1 is mostly Jason**. You only control Lucia in a few story missions, such as *Day Release* and the *Tourist Trap* trio at the end of the chapter.
- You first explore the open world as Lucia **after Chapter 1 ends**.
- **Free switching** unlocks in **Chapter 2**. PowerPyx places it after the main mission *Coffee Break*.

# How to switch

**Hold D-pad Right**, then press **R1 / RB**. You can also press **Triangle / Y** in the same menu to arrange to meet up.

A character shows **greyed out** when they can't be selected right now. That can happen because they're busy with a mission, at home, or have just called a RydeMe to leave. Some missions also require a specific character, which is shown on the mission's map icon.

# Stats

Each character has three stats, and each stat has **four levels**:

| Stat | What it does | How to train it |
|---|---|---|
| Health | Your health bar | Weights and weight machines |
| Stamina | Sprinting and exertion | Running, cycling, swimming, treadmills |
| Focus | How long Combat Focus (slow motion) lasts | Fishing, pool, golf, shooting ranges, even petting animals |

- **Finish a workout** or you lose its gains. Protein afterwards speeds up recovery.
- In Chapter 1, Jason's weights are on the **terrace of his house**. Gyms charge a membership fee.
- Food and drink give **temporary** buffs. Water plus an apple boosts all three stats.
- PowerPyx's quickest Focus training: at **Rodgers Ranch & Shooting Range**, on Port Gellhorn's north-east edge, buy the $5 waiver and repeat the *Market Range Beginner* challenge.
- Your Criminal Profile also decides what trains Focus. Professional and Hardened characters improve it with non-lethal takedowns, while Violent and Psycho characters improve it with headshots. See [Wanted & Heat](/guides/gta-6-wanted-heat/).

# Relationship levels

The romance between Jason and Lucia is **optional**. Their relationship doesn't affect whether they'll work together, but it can change how the story plays out. There are four levels:

1. Running on Empty
2. Partners in Crime
3. Crazy in Love
4. Hot Together

Spending time together, holding hands and dancing raise the level. Ignoring your partner's calls or texts, or failing what they ask during missions, lowers it. Check the level by holding **D-pad Right** or in the map's **Jason & Lucia** tab (L1 / LB).

# Working as a team

When the two of you are together, the D-pad Right menu lets you hand over weapons or stash bags. You can also send your partner to sell goods to a fence. Put the bag in your personal car's trunk first, and note that PowerPyx says this option isn't always available.
