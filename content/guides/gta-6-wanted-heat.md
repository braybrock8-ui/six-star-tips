GTA 6's police don't just chase a dot on the map. They search for a **description** of you, your clothes and your car. Once you understand that, escaping gets much easier.

# The six stars and what their colours mean

GTA 6 has **six wanted stars**. The colour of the stars tells you what the police know.

| Stars | Meaning |
|---|---|
| White outline | Police know a crime happened, but not that it was you |
| Solid white | They know it was you and they can see you. You're being chased |
| Grey | They know it was you but have lost sight of you. They're searching |
| Red outline | A crime happened here, but you no longer match the description |
| Solid red | They know it was you and you've lost them, but local cops are on high alert for your current look |

# Search zones

When police respond, a **search circle** appears where the crime happened.

- **Blue** means you haven't been linked to the crime. **Red** means you have.
- Being seen in the **inner circle** almost guarantees trouble. The fainter **outer ring** gives you more room to slip away.
- While police can see you, the inner circle moves with you until you break line of sight.

# Heat: what the police know about you

Red round **Heat icons** under the stars show what police know about you, such as your vehicle, your appearance or your weapon. To lose Heat, change whatever they've identified. Heat also fades by itself if you stay out of sight long enough.

Police build that description from **witnesses** and **security cameras**. Crimes nobody sees may never be reported.

# How to lose the cops

1. **Break line of sight and leave the search circle.**
2. **Change your look.** You can store spare outfits in your car's trunk. Add them at a wardrobe (Outfits tab, Square / X), then change from the weapon wheel while in the car (R1 / RB, hanger icon). Taking off a bandana, glasses or hat from the item wheel also helps.
3. **Swap vehicles**, especially if your car has been reported.
4. **Split up.** If police are looking for a couple, Jason and Lucia going separate ways breaks the description.
5. **Stop the call.** Press Square / X on a witness to slap their phone away before they reach the police.
6. **Pay 'n' Spray.** PowerPyx recommends a respray *after* you've escaped the search area, to shed Heat on your car.

> [!TIP] From 4 stars, helicopters join the chase. PowerPyx suggests following wilderness train tracks (south-west of Vice City, or north of Ambrosia, which has a tunnel), since police cars rarely spawn there. A fast motorcycle that weaves through traffic also works well after robberies.

# Cars with trackers

Newer cars have **trackers** that you can't switch off. **Advanced trackers** report your location to police whenever you stop moving. Scan a car with the WAiNK app (hold D-pad Up) before you steal it so you know what you're getting.

# Busted or Wasted

If you're Busted or Wasted, you lose **all the loose cash you're carrying** and any **unowned weapons** (guns picked up from enemies). Money in BuckMe and guns you've bought are safe.

# Your Criminal Profile

Jason and Lucia each have their own **Criminal Profile**. It moves along a scale of five levels based on how you play:

| Profile | How you get there | What changes |
|---|---|---|
| Professional | Stealth, no casualties, clean getaways | Non-lethal takedowns train Focus |
| Hardened | Wounding (yellow hit markers) instead of killing | Non-lethal takedowns train Focus |
| Violent | Headshots, killing enemies and bystanders | Headshot kills train Focus |
| Psycho | Mass killing of police and civilians, killing pets | Cops are more likely to shoot on sight, and PowerPyx reports you can't surrender |
| Irredeemable | Long-term extreme violence | **Permanent.** You can't change it back |

> [!WARN] Irredeemable is locked for good once you reach it. If you like options, don't drift past Psycho.

You can see each character's profile in the **Jason & Lucia** tab of the map (L1 / LB), or by holding **D-pad Right** in-game.
