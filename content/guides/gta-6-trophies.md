GTA 6 has **55 trophies**: 1 Platinum, 2 Gold, 6 Silver and 46 Bronze. On Xbox there are 55 achievements worth **1,000 Gamerscore**. The Platinum is **Vice Got a Price!**. Every award can be earned in story mode, and none of them involve online play.

> [!TIP] The two biggest grinds are **A Long Road** (100% completion) and **The Shootist** (gold in all 42 shooting range challenges). The shooting ranges also train Focus and unlock ammo cheats, so start them early.

# Hidden and secret trophies

These descriptions are deliberately cryptic in-game:

- **In The Beginning…**: enjoy a cocktail with a view on Ocean Beach.
- **Capital Offense**: smash your way out of a vehicle showroom in classic style.
- **Let's Go Bowling**: follow through at the Kolt & Associates bowling alley.
- **Ten Pin**: bowl 'em over with a melee drive-by.
- **Catch Me If You Can**: make an unscheduled departure at VCIA.
- **Personal Best**, **Bad Luck, I Guess** and **The Truth Is Out There**: cryptic hints only, no details published yet.
