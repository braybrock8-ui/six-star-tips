# Is there a 60fps or performance mode?

No. Rockstar says GTA 6 **targets 30 frames per second** on every console, pushing both platforms "to their absolute limits" to keep Leonida's density and detail. Rockstar hasn't announced a performance mode for any console, and Digital Foundry's analysis found none.

# How each console runs it

This table is based on Rockstar's own description plus Digital Foundry's analysis, as reported by several outlets.

| Console | Resolution | Frame rate | Notes |
|---|---|---|---|
| PS5 Pro | 1440p internal, upscaled to 4K with PSSR | 30fps, the most stable | Ray-traced shadows on PS5 Pro only. Digital Foundry rates it the best way to play |
| PS5 | 1440p internal | 30fps, dips in heavy action | Reports put the worst stress-test dips around 23fps |
| Xbox Series X | Not reported yet | 30fps | Digital Foundry rates it level with the base PS5 |
| Xbox Series S | 720p internal, upscaled to 1080p | Mid-20s, about 20 in the heaviest action | The weakest version by a distance |

On PS5, Rockstar highlights DualSense haptics (adjustable in accessibility settings), Tempest 3D Audio support and improved load times.

# Cloud and Remote Play

- **Xbox Cloud Gaming:** you can stream GTA 6 on Xbox Series X|S and **Xbox One** consoles. It uses Xbox Series S graphics settings.
- **Remote Play** is fully supported on both PlayStation and Xbox.

# Fix: washed-out or faded screenshots

This is an HDR issue. Kotaku published these fixes:

- **PS5:** Settings → Screen and Video → Video Output → turn HDR off.
- **Xbox:** turn off **Allow HDR10** and **Auto HDR**, then restart the console. The restart matters.

# Settings worth changing

- **Brightness:** nights in GTA 6 are very dark. Run **Settings → Display → Brightness Calibration** before you start.
- **GPS voice:** Settings → Audio → **GPS Voice** controls spoken directions.
- **Jump button:** jump is **Cross / A** by default, and you can remap it in the options. You set walking speed by clicking the left stick.
- **Subtitles:** turn them on. Plenty of mission instructions come over gunfire or the radio.

# Accessibility options

- **Menu Narration**, **Cutscene Audio Descriptions** and **High Contrast Mode** (no colour customisation) are all included.
- **Aim assist** settings are available.
- PowerPyx points to **Accessible Combat** and **Reduced Vehicle Damage** under Settings → Gameplay. They change the game's balance a lot, so you may want them only for hard missions or collectible clean-up.

# Download size and patches

- GTA 6 needs **up to 212 GB** of free space, depending on your console.
- Red Bull reports two pre-launch patches:
  - **01.002.000** (Nov 13): about 1.07 GB on PS5 and 2.18 GB on Xbox
  - **01.002.001** (Nov 16): about 602 MB on PS5
- Rockstar hasn't published patch notes for either. Leave automatic updates on.
