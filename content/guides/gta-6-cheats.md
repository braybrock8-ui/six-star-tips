GTA 6 has **61 cheats**, but they work differently from older games. You unlock them by finishing the story and then meeting a requirement for each one. Using them also has costs. Rockstar hasn't published a list, so we compared three independent cheat lists and only show a code as confirmed when at least two of them match exactly.

> [!WARN] Using a cheat **turns off saving** for that session. PowerPyx and Red Bull both report that it also **disables trophies and achievements**. Save first.

# How to enter a cheat

1. Press **D-pad Up** to open your phone.
2. On PS5, hold **L1** and press **R1**. On Xbox, hold **LB** and press **RB**.
3. Enter the sequence on the D-pad. The codes are the same on both consoles.

Cheats only work **after you finish the story**, and only once that cheat's own requirement is met. Entering a locked cheat does nothing. PowerPyx reports that unlocked cheats are listed under **Progress menu → Cheats**. Unlike GTA 5, there are no phone-number cheats.

# Disputed codes

The lists disagree on these, so we don't treat any version as confirmed yet:

- **Raise Wanted Level.** Two lists give different sequences and the third has none. Unlock: lose a 3-star wanted level 50 times.
- **Drunk.** Only one list has a code. Unlock: spend $2,500 on alcohol.
- **Unlimited Focus** and **Cycle Weather** also appear with slightly different sequences in Dexerto's list. The versions above are the ones Red Bull and PowerPyx agree on.
