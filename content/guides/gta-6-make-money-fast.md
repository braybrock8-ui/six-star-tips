Money in GTA 6 opens up with the story. Better fences, tools and robberies unlock chapter by chapter, so the fastest route to big money is mostly to keep playing. Below is what pays best at each stage. Every payout here is backed by at least two guides from people who've played the released game, unless a single source is named.

> [!WARN] Bank your cash. If you get Busted or Wasted you drop everything you're carrying, and GTABoom reports that carried cash is wiped at the end of Chapter 1. Open BuckMe through the **Buck Me** main mission and deposit often.

# Quick answer

| Stage | Best method | Typical payout |
|---|---|---|
| Chapter 1 | Illegal Operations, plus the lost drug packages | Up to about $2,000 per camp, about $900 per package |
| Chapter 2 | Sell trackerless cars to Moses | About $1,000 to $3,000 per car |
| Chapter 3 | Jewelry stores for Yasmani | $10,000 to $25,000 per robbery |
| Chapter 3 | Randy's wish-list supercars | $30,000+ per car |
| End of Chapter 4 | Large bank vaults | Up to $205,000 per vault |

# Chapter 1

**Illegal Operations.** The first one most players find is **Kingfishers: Fishing Shacks**, north-west of Key Lento. Loot every enemy and every stash of product. A full clear is worth close to $2,000. A house on the dock on the camp's west side also holds a free Unique Weapon Variant.

**Strange Noises at Three Sheets liquor (Key Lento).** First finish Shirley's errand to buy Salty Squid rum. Then go behind the store, clear out the armed guards and loot the hidden stash. If you investigate before finishing Shirley's errand, the shop locks down for a long time.

**Find the lost drugs.** After the main mission **Clean Up After Donnie**, Cal marks four locations around the Keys. Each one holds about $900 of product, and guards are light. You can carry two duffel bags, which is enough to grab all four sites in one trip. Bring an off-road vehicle.

**Crashed plane near Hamlet.** Look for smoke in a field south of Hamlet. It has a pistol and sellable drugs. An armed truck shows up soon after you loot it.

**Kenton's treasure dives.** These start after the main mission **Island Dreams**, which gives you scuba gear. Kenton only pays after he sells what you find, which can take a few hours. Early wrecks are worth about $1,400.

**Sell to Brian or Donnie.** Hover over their map icons to compare prices. One often pays noticeably more than the other.

> [!TIP] Store stick-ups (aim at the clerk, loot the till) pay very little. They're fine in an emergency but not worth chasing.

# Chapter 2: Port Gellhorn

**Moses buys stolen cars.** He unlocks after the main mission **Moses**.

- He only takes cars **without a tracker**. Scan a car with the WAiNK app (hold D-pad Up) to check before you steal it.
- He pays roughly **$1,000 to $3,000** per car, and his garage reopens after about two minutes.
- Vice reports no daily limit.
- He refuses cars stolen too close to his garage, so grab them from Port Gellhorn's main road instead.

**Dunk** becomes a second fence after the main mission **Slam Dunk**.

**La Cascara Motel** is an Illegal Operation in Port Gellhorn that Vice rates among the best earners in this chapter.

# Chapter 3: Vice City

**Jewelry stores.** These start with the main mission **Yasmani's Jewelry Jobs**. Each robbery pays **$10,000 to $25,000**, and you can hit several stores in a row. Try for the backroom safe, and leave before the cops arrive. Yasmani also pays well for stolen jewelry.

**Sell cars at Pay 'n' Spray.** Early in Chapter 3, Pay 'n' Spray starts buying cars with standard trackers. A good one is worth roughly $2,500 to $4,000+.

**Randy's wish list.** The main mission **Clonin' Keys, Makin' GS** gives you the Key Cloner, which PowerPyx puts at about 40 hours in. Randy then buys high-end cars from his wish list for **$30,000+** each, as long as they're in perfect condition.

**Small banks and payday loans.** These unlock after the main mission **Credit Check**, which also gives you the **Dye Pack Jammer** you need to sell marked bills. Payouts run about **$5,000 to $45,000**.

# End of Chapter 4: bank vaults

The optional mission **That's Where The Money Is… (Wait at the Cafe)** appears at the very end of Chapter 4 and unlocks the large bank vaults.

- Follow a security van to a bank. When a guard opens the vault, follow them inside.
- Vaults pay **up to $205,000**. PowerPyx calls them by far the best money in the game.
- Robbing a vault gives you an **instant 4-star wanted level**. A fast motorcycle that can cut through traffic, then a quick vehicle swap, is the recommended escape.
- After you've robbed two payday loans, Yasmani sells tips on security vans for $10,000.

# Every fence and what they pay

Payout ratings below are from PowerPyx's fence guide. They tell you who pays best for each type of stolen goods.

| Fence | Where | When | Narcotics | Jewelry | Marked bills |
|---|---|---|---|---|---|
| Brian | South of Key Lento | After *Lab Fees*, gone after Chapter 1 | Very low | Very low | Very low |
| Donnie | South of Key Lento | Gone after *Swamped* | Low | Very low | Very low |
| Dunk | Port Gellhorn | After *Slam Dunk* | Low | Low | Very low |
| Tyrone | La Perle, Vice City | Once you reach Vice City | High | Medium | Medium |
| Yasmani | Little Cuba, Vice City | Chapter 3 | Medium | High | High |
| Moses | Port Gellhorn | After *Moses* | Cars without trackers | | |
| Randy | Balsero, Vice City | After *Clonin' Keys, Makin' GS* | Wish-list supercars | | |

> [!TIP] From Chapter 3, after you unlock Yasmani, you can drop stolen goods at your garage and choose which fence gets them.
