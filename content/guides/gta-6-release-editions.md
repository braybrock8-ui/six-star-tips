# Release

- **Out now:** November 19, 2026, worldwide on **PS5 and Xbox Series X|S**.
- The game unlocked at **midnight local time** in each country, so some regions got it hours before others.
- Pre-load opened on **November 10**. On PS5 it opened at midnight local time, and on Xbox at 12 a.m. GMT.

# Editions and price

| Edition | Price (US) | What's in it |
|---|---|---|
| Standard | $79.99 | The game |
| Ultimate | $99.99 | The game plus Ultimate Edition content |

**Ultimate Edition content, as listed by Rockstar:**

- '95 Grotti Cheetah
- Hawk & Little Morgan Revolvers
- Personalized Weapon Variants
- Vice City Style
- Jason's Safehouse Vehicles
- Ganado Retro Build
- Rideout Customs Mod Shop
- Sara's Unisex Salon
- Shitzu Squalo
- Stock 305 Clothing Store
- '67 Vapid Dominator Buggy
- Electric Fang Tattoo Parlor
- One-Eyed Willie's Mod Shop
- Goodtime Gear
- PTT Youngin$ Compound
- Classic Car Collection

Rockstar says this content is "threaded across" the story, with **new items unlocking in each chapter**. Standard owners can buy the **Ultimate Edition Upgrade** at any time from the PlayStation or Microsoft store. There is no physical Ultimate Edition.

# Pre-order bonus: ends today

> [!WARN] Digital purchases of either edition made **before November 20, 2026** include the **Vintage Vice City Pack** and **one month of GTA+**. Rockstar's launch post says today, November 19, is the last day.

The Vintage Vice City Pack includes:

- a '55 Vapid Stanier Sedan and garage
- outfits and hairstyles
- an exclusive weapon pattern

Physical pre-orders get the pack but not the GTA+ month.

# Physical copies have no disc

The boxed version contains a **download code**, not a disc. PlayStation codes are region-locked to your account's region, and Xbox codes aren't. Japanese codes expire 170 days after launch.

# Download size

GTA 6 needs **up to 212 GB** of free space, depending on your console. Day-one patches come on top of that.

# Streaming

- **Xbox Cloud Gaming:** stream on Xbox Series X|S and Xbox One. It uses Series S graphics settings.
- **Remote Play** is supported on PlayStation and Xbox.

# Is GTA 6 on PC?

**Not yet, and Rockstar hasn't announced a PC version.** A Raymond James analyst told CBS News that PC versions have historically come about 12 to 18 months after console. Treat any specific PC date as rumour.

# Is there GTA 6 Online?

**Not at launch.** GTA 6 is single-player. Rockstar hasn't announced an online mode, a name or a date. Twitch's CEO told Bloomberg he expects the next GTA Online in 2027, but Rockstar hasn't confirmed that.

# Reviews

- **Metacritic: 99**, tying *The Legend of Zelda: Ocarina of Time* as the highest-rated game ever. That figure was based on about 150 reviews on launch day, and scores may still move.
- **IGN: 10/10. GameSpot: 10/10.** Eurogamer, GamesRadar+ and Giant Bomb all gave it 5 out of 5.

# Sales so far

- Sensor Tower estimates nearly **16 million pre-orders**, worth about **$1.5 billion**, which it calls the most pre-ordered game ever.
- Kotaku's launch-day figure is **over 17 million copies sold** and **$1.65 billion** projected.

These are third-party estimates. Rockstar hasn't released official numbers yet.

# Music

- **Grand Theft Auto VI: The Album**, 34 original tracks, is out tonight at midnight ET on streaming services, vinyl and CD.
- A 30-track **Original Score** by Justice is due December 11.
- In-game radio is the biggest in the series, and Jason and Lucia also have on-demand podcasts.

# Rating

The ESRB rating is Mature. Rockstar lists these content descriptors: Blood and Gore, Intense Violence, Mature Humor, Nudity, Strong Language, Strong Sexual Content, and Use of Drugs and Alcohol. The game also has In-Game Purchases.
