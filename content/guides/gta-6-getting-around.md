# Fast travel

You **can't fast travel by clicking the map**. Instead you ride in a taxi and pay to skip the trip:

1. Set a waypoint on the map. It works even for areas you haven't discovered.
2. **Chapter 1:** call **Paradise Taxi** from your phone's contacts. Taxis are available once you've finished *Gone Fishing*.
3. **Chapter 2 onwards:** use the **RydeMe** app, which is quicker.
4. Get in, hold **L2 / LT** to talk to the driver, then press **Triangle / Y** to pay **$100** to skip the ride. The $100 is the same for any distance and comes on top of the normal fare.

> [!TIP] PowerPyx's quickest way to clear the map fog is to take a taxi to an airfield, steal a plane and fly around. They say it takes about 2 to 3 hours for the whole map. Planes and helicopters need fuel too.

# Saving

PowerPyx reports how saving works:

- Save manually from the pause menu: **Game → Save Game**. You can't save during most missions.
- There are **15 manual save slots** and **1 autosave**.
- There's **no mission replay** and no mission gold medals, so keep a manual save at the start of each chapter.

> [!WARN] Activating a cheat disables saving for that session. Save before you use one.

# Fuel, damage and repairs

- Every vehicle has a **fuel gauge** and a **body condition** meter. Electric cars use charging stations.
- To repair a vehicle, use a **Pay 'n' Spray** (which also repaints), a **Mod Shop** (each one specialises in certain vehicle types), or pull up to a **gas pump** and choose Services → Repair.
- Tolls cost **$5**, unless you're in a stolen or borrowed vehicle.
- To flip a car, walk into it to push, and **hold Square / X** for a big shove. This also works on small boats stuck on land.

# Stealing cars

- **Older cars:** a hotwire minigame using both sticks. The sweet spots usually mirror each other, so move the sticks symmetrically.
- **Newer cars** need electronic keys. You get the **Key Cloner** in Chapter 3. Until then, pull drivers out of running cars.
- **Trackers** can't be turned off. **Advanced trackers** call the police whenever you stop moving. Scan a car with **WAiNK** (hold D-pad Up) to check before you take it.

# Your own vehicles

- Jason starts with his Ganado, a boat moored behind the house, and, on the Ultimate Edition, a Dinka '72 Enduro motorcycle under the house.
- His Key Lento home has **no space to register extra cars**. More storage arrives in **Chapter 2 after you meet Moses**.
- PowerPyx reports that from Chapter 2 you can **register stolen cars** as personal vehicles and **summon them with the Scooter Bros app**. Registering 10 earns the *On the Spot* trophy.
- After the main mission *Just a Scratch*, you can call **Joey** to bring your car to you.
- There are **8 garage properties** to buy. Owning all of them earns the *Little Boxes* trophy.
